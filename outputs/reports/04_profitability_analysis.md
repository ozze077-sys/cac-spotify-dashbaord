# Phase 4: Profitability Analysis

## Q3. Is Spotify becoming more profitable? **Yes, clearly.**

| Year | Gross margin % | Operating margin % | Opex / revenue % | S&M / revenue % | FCF margin % | Incremental op. margin % |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2021 | 26.8 | 1.0 | 25.8 | n/a | 2.9 | n/a |
| 2022 | 25.0 | -5.6 | 30.6 | n/a | 0.2 | -36.6 |
| 2023 | 25.6 | -3.4 | 29.0 | 11.6 | 5.1 | 14.0 |
| 2024 | 30.1 | 8.7 | 21.4 | 8.9 | 14.6 | 74.6 |
| 2025 | 32.0 | 12.8 | 19.2 | 8.3 | 16.7 | 55.1 |

- Gross margin: 25.0% (2022 low) -> **32.0%** (2025), +7.0pp.
- Operating margin: -5.6% -> **12.8%**;
  operating income swung from a €659M loss to a €2,198M profit.
- Free cash flow: €21M (2022) -> **€2,874M** (2025),
  a 16.7% FCF margin.
- Operating leverage: in 2025, **55%** of incremental revenue reached
  operating income (in 2024 it was 75%, helped by cost cuts). Total
  opex fell in absolute terms in both 2024 and 2025.

## Q4 (part 1). Is marketing spend growing faster than revenue? **No.**

- S&M fell from 11.6% of revenue (2023) to **8.3%** (2025).
- 2025 S&M grew 2.4% against revenue growth of 9.7%.
- S&M is 43% of 2025 operating expenses, so it is a major lever on operating margin.

- Free cash flow is a non-IFRS, company-defined measure.
