# Phase 3a: Revenue Analysis

## Annual revenue

| Year | Revenue (EUR M) | Total growth % | Premium growth % | Ad growth % | Premium mix % |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2021 | 9,668 | n/a | n/a | n/a | 87.5 |
| 2022 | 11,727 | 21.3 | 21.2 | 22.2 | 87.4 |
| 2023 | 13,247 | 13.0 | 12.8 | 13.9 | 87.3 |
| 2024 | 15,673 | 18.3 | 19.5 | 10.3 | 88.2 |
| 2025 | 17,186 | 9.7 | 11.1 | -1.0 | 89.3 |

- Total revenue CAGR 2021-2025: **15.5%** (premium 16.1%,
  ad-supported 11.0%).
- Revenue growth slowed from 18.3% in 2024 to 9.7% in 2025.
- Ad-supported revenue **fell 1.0%** in 2025, so premium delivered
  101% of that year's incremental revenue.
- Premium mix rose from 87.5% to 89.3%. Spotify's revenue is
  increasingly concentrated in subscriptions.

## Quarterly trend (2024-Q4 to 2025-Q4)

| Quarter | Revenue (EUR M) | QoQ % | Ad QoQ % | Premium mix % |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2024-Q4 | 4,242 | n/a | n/a | 87.3 |
| 2025-Q1 | 4,190 | -1.2 | -22.0 | 90.0 |
| 2025-Q2 | 4,193 | 0.1 | 8.1 | 89.2 |
| 2025-Q3 | 4,272 | 1.9 | -1.5 | 89.6 |
| 2025-Q4 | 4,531 | 6.1 | 16.1 | 88.6 |

- Q4 2025 vs Q4 2024: total revenue **+6.8%**; ad-supported revenue **-3.5%**.
- The Q1 drop in ad revenue is seasonal: Q4 is the peak advertising quarter.
- Reported growth is in EUR. Part of the 2025 slowdown may come from currency effects, but this dataset has no
  constant-currency figures. Check the shareholder deck before attributing the slowdown to demand.
