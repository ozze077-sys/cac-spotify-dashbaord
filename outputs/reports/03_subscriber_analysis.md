# Phase 3b: Revenue & Subscriber Economics

## Q1. Is revenue growing faster than the subscriber base?

| Year | Premium subs (M) | Net adds (M) | Sub growth % | Revenue growth % | Gap (pp) | Monthly ARPU (EUR) | Ad rev / ad MAU / yr (EUR) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2022 | 205 | 25.00 | 13.89 | 21.30 | 7.41 | 4.44 | 5.56 |
| 2023 | 236 | 31.00 | 15.12 | 12.96 | -2.16 | 4.37 | 4.99 |
| 2024 | 263 | 27.00 | 11.44 | 18.31 | 6.87 | 4.62 | 4.61 |
| 2025 | 290 | 27.00 | 10.27 | 9.65 | -0.61 | 4.63 | 4.08 |

- **Mixed:** revenue outgrew premium subscribers in
  2 of 4 years (2022, 2024).
- 2021-2025 cumulative: revenue 78%
  vs premium subscribers 61%
  vs MAU 85%.
- In 2025, revenue growth (9.7%) fell just below subscriber growth
  (10.3%) because ad revenue declined. Premium revenue alone grew
  11.1%.

## Q2. How much revenue does Spotify generate per Premium subscriber?

- 2025 approximate monthly premium ARPU: **€4.63**
  (€56 per year), up from €4.44 in 2022.
- ARPU dipped in 2023 (-1.5%) and recovered in 2024
  (+5.6%), consistent with the 2023-2024 price increases.
  Subscriber mix (family, duo, student and emerging-market plans) and FX may also contribute; this dataset cannot
  separate the effects.
- Ad-supported monetisation is far lower: about €4.08 per ad-supported MAU per **year**.

The 2025 average of the quarterly figures (€4.65) is close to the annual
approximation, which supports using the annual method.

## Method notes

- Monthly ARPU = premium revenue / average of opening and closing subscribers / 12 (or / 3 for quarters).
- A two-point average understates intra-year timing, so treat these figures as approximate.
- Premium ARPU covers premium revenue only. Ad revenue earned from free-tier users is excluded.
